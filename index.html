About Us

Marcoso Global Resources Ltd is a trading company with a combination of 25+ years of industry experience.

arrow_circle_right

We source high quality and specific grades of special steels from our internationally recognised and renowned steel producing partners to supply and service our vast international clientele base.

arrow_circle_right

Some of these products include; Electrolytic tinplate, chromium coated steel (tin free steel) and Protact (laminated steel). We can also source hot rolled steel, cold rolled steel, black plate and galvanised steel

arrow_circle_right

All logistics related to the buying and selling of our products is kept in house. With this approach we have established strong relationships within the freight forwarding and shipping industries, allowing us to offer bespoke logistical support/resolutions to others.
